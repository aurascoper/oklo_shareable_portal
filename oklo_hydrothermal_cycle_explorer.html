1. INFERRED REACTOR CYCLING
Meshik et al. (2004, PRL 93:182302): Trapped fission-product Xe isotopes infer ~30 min active fission separated by ~2.5 h dormancy (180 min period, 60 frames). The isotope record does not directly show a surface geyser erupting; pulsing/flow timing remains model-dependent (Ibekwe et al., 2020).
2. MINERALOGICAL CAVEAT
Oklo Sudoite/Chlorite vs Montmorillonite: Oklo hydrothermal clays include di-trioctahedral chlorites such as sudoite and illite (Pourcelot & Gauthier-Lafaye, 1998, Chem. Geol. 145:103-115, doi:10.1016/S0009-2541(98)00194-6). Laboratory montmorillonite (Ferris et al., 1996, Nature 381:59-61, doi:10.1038/381059a0, up to 55-mers) is a prebiotic analogue, not verified Oklo core gangue.
3. ABIOTIC FIREWALL & SCALE SEPARATION
Abiotic H_5 Only (Zero Cellular EPS): Evaluates whether abiotic oligomer synthesis outpaces thermal hydrolysis, radiolysis, and washout. Cellular EPS/cell-wall bridging belongs strictly to the separate modern biofilm model (H_6). Macroscopic (m), pore (um), and molecular (nm) scales are strictly separated.
